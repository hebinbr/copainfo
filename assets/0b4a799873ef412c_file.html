What's Happening Around the Company
Related Argent launches Ferry Island Build-to-Rent residence in Tottenham Hale

On June 4, Related Argent unveiled Ferry Island, a new Build-to-Rent (BtR) residence that has completed the company's 1,000-home Tottenham Hale master plan in London. Designed by Allford Hall Monaghan Morris Architects and featuring interiors by Conran and Partners, Ferry Island offers 484 high-quality residences across two buildings, ranging from one- to three-bedroom homes. Residents benefit from a retail offering, adjacent location to the Tottenham Hale underground station and 15,000 square feet of amenity space managed by Related Argent, which includes two roof terraces, a gym and fitness suite, co-working lounges, a screening room and dedicated social spaces.

Related Midwest and Advocate Health Care celebrate hospital groundbreaking at Quantum Shore

On June 2, Related Midwest joined Advocate Health and the South Chicago community to commemorate the groundbreaking of Advocate Health's new hospital at Quantum Shore Chicago—the first hospital built in the community in over 100 years. The state-of-the-art facility will offer high-quality health care and will create new opportunities for residents and their families, representing an important step forward for the community.

Learn More
Related Digital and partners celebrate Stargate data center construction in Saline Township, MI

On June 1, Related Digital hosted Governor Gretchen Whitmer and its project partners, Oracle, OpenAI, Blackstone and Walbridge, to celebrate construction of the Saline data center project—the single largest economic investment in Michigan's history. The team also announced a new commitment from the project partners to the community: a $10 million contribution to renovate and expand the Saline Recreation Center, building on $14 million already committed to local investments in Saline Township.

Related Texas unveils new vision for One Lady Bird Lake luxury residence in Austin

On May 29, Related Texas revealed new renderings and details for One Lady Bird Lake, a new luxury residential development currently under construction in Austin's South Central Waterfront District. Located along the shores of Lady Bird Lake, the 18-story development will feature 101 luxury condominium residences and 197 luxury rental residences, bringing a total of 298 homes to one of Austin's most sought-after waterfront locations. The development will also include two full-service restaurants and thoughtfully curated amenity spaces. Residents will enjoy unobstructed waterfront views and direct access to one of Austin's most dynamic and rapidly evolving neighborhoods.

Celebrating new housing milestones at Willets Point in Queens

On May 18, Queens Development Group (Related and Sterling Equities) alongside our partners at City Hall, NYC Department of Housing Preservation & Development, New York City Housing Development Corporation, New York City Economic Development Corporation, Wells Fargo and Goldman Sachs celebrated the opening of 880 affordable homes at Willets Point Commons. Related Midwest advancing affordable housing in Ann Arbor, MI with 350 S. Fifth Avenue

On May 19, Related Midwest and the Ann Arbor Housing Commission announced that work will soon begin on 350 S. Fifth Avenue, Michigan’s largest all-affordable residential development in more than 40 years. When complete, the 20-story community will transform a brownfield site into 330 much-needed affordable residences for low- and moderate-income households, placing residents steps from public transit, jobs and amenities throughout downtown.

Terry Manor Apartments: Affordable Senior Living Renovation

In this video, we highlight the recently completed renovation of Terry Manor Apartments, an affordable senior living community adjacent to the USC campus in Los Angeles. Residents have been welcomed into fully renovated apartments, enhanced common areas featuring a curated art collection and thoughtfully designed green spaces that foster connection and well-being. Through our partnership with Rainbow Housing, a national organization dedicated to resident services and supportive programming, the community also benefits from resources that help residents thrive both at home and beyond.

Vision and Impact

Related CEO Jeff Blau shares vision for Hudson Yards expansion in Crain’s New York Business

On May 13, Related Companies CEO Jeff Blau discusses the importance of the public and private sector working together to enable investment that creates real benefits for New York City. The plan for the next phase of Hudson Yards would do exactly that by delivering up to 4,000 new homes, including at least 625 permanently affordable apartments, 35,000 union construction jobs, 6.6 acres of public open space, a new K-8 public school and more. It’s time to build the next chapter of New York.

South Shore on The Park residence unveils mosaic art installation

In May, Related Midwest, Related Affordable Foundation and After School Matters celebrated the unveiling of affordable residence South Shore on The Park's new mosaic art installation, which was created by Chicago teens in partnership with Green Star Movement. 18 students from the South Shore community worked together to craft the vibrant mosaics, representing the property's second art project completed by talented local young people.
